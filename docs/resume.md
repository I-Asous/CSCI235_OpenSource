## Make it easy to find

1. **Link your merged PR** in your `contributors/<you>.md` file, and keep the link handy for applications.
2. **Pin the repo** on your GitHub profile: Profile → Customize your pins → choose your `CSCI235` fork.
3. **Write a profile README:** create a repo named exactly your username and add a `README.md`. Mention this project.
4. **LinkedIn:** add it under *Projects* with the repo link.

## Be ready to talk about it

Interviewers love "tell me about a project" questions. Prepare a 60-second answer:

- **What** did you build, and what's its time complexity?
- **A hard part:** a bug, a memory leak the sanitizer caught, a tricky edge case.
- **Feedback:** something a reviewer asked you to change, and what you learned from it.
- **Process:** how you kept your branch up to date and handled a conflict.
